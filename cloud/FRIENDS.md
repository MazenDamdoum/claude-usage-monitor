# Claude Usage, for the friend whose account is shared

Someone is using your Claude account through Claude Usage Monitor. This app on your iPhone shows
**your account only** and lets you decide how it's used.
In this guide, "Sam" stands for the person who shared the app with you; you'll see their name.

## Set it up (2 minutes)

1. Open the link you were sent in **Safari** (not Chrome, not inside a chat app).
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Claude Usage** from your Home Screen. It signs you in by itself.
4. Tap **Enable Notifications** and allow them.

The link works once. If it says it was already used or has expired, ask for a new one. If you had
an older Claude Usage icon, delete it first, or you'll get every notification twice.

## What you see

- Your 5-hour and weekly usage, live, with when each one resets.
- Whether your account is being used right now.
- About how many more Opus prompts fit before the limit.

## What you're notified about

Each of these can be turned off at the bottom of the app.

- **Heads-up:** "Sam may switch to you soon", about 15–30 minutes before your account is used.
- **Start and stop:** when your account starts or stops being used.
- **Usage:** at 25%, 50%, 75%, 85% and when the limit is reached.
- **Resets:** 1 hour, 30, 15 and 5 minutes before a limit resets, and when it does.

## What you control

- **Allow Sam to use it:** turn it off and Sam's PC moves off your account at once and stays
  off until you turn it back on.
- **5-hour cap / weekly cap:** Sam's PC stops using your account when it reaches that
  percentage. Sam can also set a cap of his own. The lower of the two applies.

Tap any notification to open the app on your current usage.

## Privacy

The app shows your account's usage numbers and nothing else: no conversations, no other
accounts, no emails. Your login stays on the PC it's used from, never in the app or the cloud.
To stop, tap **Unpair this iPhone** at the bottom of the app and delete it from your Home Screen.
